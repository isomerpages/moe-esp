<p><strong>What Is the ESP?</strong>
</p>
<p>The ESP stands as the educational cornerstone of the Singapore Green Plan
2030.&nbsp; While environmental education has long been a part of Singapore's
schools, the ESP enhances MOE’s support and galvanises schools to adopt
a whole-school approach to nurture our students as environmental stewards.
The ESP is the next bound of sustainability education in all schools, from
primary to pre-university levels.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>What does the ESP seek to achieve?&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<p>At its core, the ESP aims to cultivate generations of Singaporeans who
not only know about global and local environmental challenges but are equipped
from a young age with the mindset, will and skills to actively contribute
to a sustainable future.&nbsp; It seeks to channel our youth's environmental
interest into hopeful and constructive action, grounded in sound values,
to realise Singapore's green ambitions.&nbsp;</p>
