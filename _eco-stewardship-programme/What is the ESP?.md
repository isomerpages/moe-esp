<h4>What is the ESP?</h4>
<p>The Eco Stewardship Programme (ESP) forms a key part of the Singapore
Green Plan 2030. The ESP builds on our schools’ experience of environmental
education over the years and goes further by adopting a whole-school approach
to nurturing stewards of the environment.</p>
<p></p>
<p>From primary to pre-university levels, the ESP inspires students, teachers,
and schools to learn and actively contribute to a sustainable future. This
includes equipping our students with the mindset, will and skills to channel
their environmental interest towards hopeful and constructive actions.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Through the ESP, we are not just teaching about the environment - we are
shaping our future eco stewards to realise Singapore’s green ambitions.</p>
