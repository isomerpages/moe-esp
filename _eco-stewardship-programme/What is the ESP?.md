<h4>MOE's Eco Stewardship Programme</h4>
<p>The Eco Stewardship Programme (ESP) forms a key part of the Singapore
Green Plan 2030. It builds on our schools’ experience of environmental
education over the years and goes further by adopting a <strong>whole-school approach</strong> to
nurturing stewards of the environment.</p>
<p>From primary to pre-university levels, the ESP inspires students, teachers,
and schools to learn and actively contribute to a sustainable future. This
includes equipping our students with the mindset, will and skills to <strong>channel their environmental interest towards hopeful and constructive actions</strong>.</p>
<p>Through the ESP, we are not just teaching about the environment - we are <strong>shaping our future eco stewards</strong> to
realise Singapore’s green ambitions.</p>
